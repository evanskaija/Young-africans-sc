HELP & SUPPORT

GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Use the route that matches your membership status. The official membership record remains the source of truth.

01How do I become an official member?

Register through a Young Africans SC authorized membership channel. The official source issues the membership number.

02Can I use Join Yanga to create MY YANGA?

No. Join Yanga and MY YANGA account setup are separate. A MY YANGA account begins only after an official membership number exists.

03What do I need for Member Login?

Use your official membership number and the password created during MY YANGA setup.

04How is ownership verified?

Verification should use an OTP sent to the contact in official membership records, or an authorized staff verification process when available.

05What if my membership number is not recognized?

Do not create another account. Request authorized staff assistance so the number can be checked against the official membership record.

06Does MY YANGA require another payment?

No. MY YANGA account access is based on an official membership number and secure ownership verification. Payment collection is not part of this portal flow.
