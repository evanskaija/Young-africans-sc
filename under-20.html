ABOUT THE PROGRAM

PREPARING FOR
THE NEXT LEVEL.

The Young Africans SC Under-20 Academy represents an important stage in the development of emerging football talent.

At this level, the focus shifts toward strengthening technical abilities, improving tactical understanding, developing physical readiness and preparing young footballers for the demands of competitive senior football.

The program emphasizes discipline, teamwork, responsibility and continuous improvement, encouraging young athletes to develop the qualities required to progress in their football careers.

OUR DIRECTION

VISION & MISSION.

Building a strong foundation for competitive football and the development of future professionals.

OUR VISION

To develop a generation of talented, disciplined and technically accomplished young footballers capable of competing at the highest levels of the game.

⌾
OUR MISSION

To provide a structured and competitive football development environment that strengthens technical, tactical, physical and mental abilities while preparing young players for the transition into senior football.

THE DEVELOPMENT MODEL

DEVELOPING COMPLETE
FOOTBALLERS.

Progress at Under-20 level requires more than individual ability. It demands technical quality, tactical awareness, physical preparation and strong character.

01

TECHNICAL EXCELLENCE

Refining passing, first touch, ball control, finishing and individual football techniques required for high-level competition.

02

TACTICAL INTELLIGENCE

Developing positional awareness, game understanding, decision-making and the ability to adapt to different tactical situations.

03

PHYSICAL PREPARATION

Supporting age-appropriate strength, conditioning, speed, endurance and physical readiness for competitive football.

04

MENTALITY & DISCIPLINE

Encouraging resilience, professionalism, concentration, teamwork and responsibility both on and off the pitch.

THE PLAYER DEVELOPMENT PATHWAY

THE ROAD TO
THE FIRST TEAM.

The Under-20 stage is an important period of preparation, helping promising young footballers develop the abilities, experience and mentality required for senior competition.

01
STAGE 01

FOUNDATION

Strengthening technical ability, football intelligence and personal discipline.

02
STAGE 02

COMPETITIVE DEVELOPMENT

Building consistency, tactical understanding and experience through structured training and competitive football.

03
STAGE 03

SENIOR READINESS

Preparing players for the physical, tactical and professional expectations of senior-level football.

Progression is based on development, performance, readiness and opportunities available within the club's football structure.

PREPARATION & PERFORMANCE

TRAINING WITH
PURPOSE.

Consistent preparation, teamwork and competitive experience are essential to developing young footballers ready for the next stage.

01

Training activities designed to strengthen individual performance, collective understanding and consistency.

Opportunities to apply football skills and tactical knowledge in challenging match environments.

03

GUIDANCE & DEVELOPMENT

LEARNING FROM
THE GAME.

Effective youth development depends on knowledgeable coaching, constructive feedback and a positive learning environment.

The Under-20 Academy aims to support young footballers through practical instruction, tactical education, performance improvement and guidance that encourages responsibility and professional growth.

  • Coaching and tactical education.
  • Performance development and feedback.
  • Discipline and professional preparation.
BEYOND THE PITCH

BUILDING CHARACTER.
SHAPING FUTURES.

01

DISCIPLINE

02

03

RESPECT
