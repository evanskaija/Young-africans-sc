GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Use the route that matches your status. Official membership records are the source of truth for numbers, ownership and access.

01How can I become a Young Africans SC member?

Complete registration through an authorized Yanga membership channel. The official source issues the membership number.

02Can Join Yanga create my MY YANGA account?

No. Join Yanga is separate from MY YANGA. Account setup starts only after official membership exists.

03What do I need for Member Login?

Use the official membership number issued to you and the password created during MY YANGA setup.

04How is ownership verified?

Use an OTP sent to the contact in official records, or request authorized staff verification when that service is available.

05What if my membership number is not recognized?

Do not create another account. Use staff support so the number can be checked against the official membership record.

06Does MY YANGA require another payment?

No. MY YANGA access is based on an official membership number and secure ownership verification. Payment collection is not part of this portal flow.
